# WorldLife — Cultural Life Simulator

A BitLife-inspired life simulation game featuring authentic country cultures — including **Malaysia 🇲🇾** as a flagship experience alongside **Singapore 🇸🇬, Japan 🇯🇵, United Kingdom 🇬🇧, United States 🇺🇸, Australia 🇦🇺, South Korea 🇰🇷, Indonesia 🇮🇩, Thailand 🇹🇭, and UAE (Dubai) 🇦🇪**.

When your character **migrates to another country**, the entire simulation dynamically transforms to match the destination country's culture, currency, cities, jobs, real estate, festivals, local activities, healthcare system, and immigration/citizenship journey!

## How to Play
1. **Instant Play (No install required):** Open `index.html` directly in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
2. **Optional Local Server:** Run `py -m http.server 8000` inside this folder and visit `http://localhost:8000`.

## Highlights
- **8 Authentic Cultural Packs:** Malaysia (flagship), Singapore, Japan, UK, USA, Australia, South Korea, and Indonesia.
- **Dynamic Migration & Adaptation System:**
  - Migrate via **Work Visa**, **Student Visa**, or **Investor Visa** (or return visa-free to countries where you hold citizenship).
  - Automatic currency conversion using realistic exchange rates (e.g., `RM` → `¥`, `S$`, `£`, `$`, `A$`, `₩`, `Rp`).
  - **Arrival Culture Shock Events** & **Cultural Adaptation Meter (0–100%)**.
  - Full **Visa → Permanent Residency (PR) → Citizenship** progression with Passport Stamps!
- **Country-Aware Life Systems:**
  - Authentic education tracks & national exams (e.g., Tadika/SK/SMK/SPM in Malaysia, PSLE/O-Levels in Singapore, Suneung in South Korea, GCSE/A-Levels in UK, SAT in USA).
  - Country-specific jobs, tax rates, retirement schemes (EPF/KWSP, CPF, 401(k), Superannuation, Nenkin), healthcare costs, cars (e.g., Perodua MyVi, Proton X50, Kei Car, Ford F-150), and real estate.
  - Local dating & long-distance family relationships, festive celebrations, and end-of-life legacy ribbons with auto-save.
